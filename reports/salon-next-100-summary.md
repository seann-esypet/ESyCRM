# Next 100 salon email review

This bounded follow-up selected 100 new Place IDs from the original CA/NY/TX directory: 34 CA, 33 NY and 33 TX. It excluded every one of the earlier 120 provider-pilot IDs, existing email rows, non-operational entries and obvious non-salon names before selection. Result: **24 source-verified general-contact candidates** for manual review, **2 booking-only public business addresses** (not general-contact yield), **12 other review-only**, **43 no verified email in this bounded search**, **16 non-grooming**, **3 inaccessible/unresolved**. There are 26 source-confirmed business mailboxes in total (25 first-party, 1 independent directory).

Of the 26 published business emails, 2 are explicitly appointment/booking addresses and were not filled into the general Email Address column. 24 other existing directory rows were filled in the review copy. An additional distinct Peninsula mobile grooming business was found, but its site directs only existing clients to email, so it was retained in the off-list evidence sheet rather than appended to the general directory. Neither original uploaded workbook nor CRM contacts changed.

Source-confirmed public business mailboxes by state (including booking-only): CA 8/34; NY 10/33; TX 8/33. These are counts within this screened, state-balanced pilot, **not a projected full-directory yield**.

Specific cautions: Uncle Sylvio's site confirms a groomer and mailbox but not this Place ID's street address, so it is review-only. Pooch N Pups has conflicting current structured-data and visible-footer addresses; the source street is independently indexed, but confirm location before use. Wig-Wagz's first-party site gives a nearby intersection/ZIP rather than the source's street pin. Booking-only emails, social snippets, and other-location mailboxes are never treated as general outreach-ready contacts.

Method: one managed public-web search with two query angles per selected listing, followed by targeted first-party or exact directory page checks, with an extra bounded raw-HTML pass on unresolved candidates where scripted text extraction missed content. No paid DataForSEO/SerpAPI provider requests were made in this phase. Managed search usage was not priced here, so this report does not claim a cost per email. No email sending, contact import, form submission, or automated Facebook scraping occurred. All decisions, source URLs and exceptions are in the workbook. State JSON reports retain search results and checked-page evidence.
