Connect these Google apps to Search services?
Connecting these Google apps helps you supercharge Personal Intelligence in Search services with new, more personalized experiences

Connected Content Apps


Google Workspace
Includes services like Gmail, Calendar, and Drive


Google Photos

Your data from Connected Content Apps is used to:

personalize your Search services experience with insights about you, your relationships, and people in your world

do things for you, like get answers based on your own content

show you ads related to your interactions with Search services, which may be personalized depending on your settings

improve Google services, including training generative AI models, for everyone

Things to know
Data shared from Connected Content Apps includes things like emails, docs, events, photos, videos, and insights drawn from them, as well as location info estimated from your content or attached to these files, which may be from device location. Data shared may relate to topics you find sensitive.

Data from Connected Content Apps is retained, shared and used according to the Google Privacy Policy and your settings (such as Search Services History), and may be human reviewed. Today, your interactions with Search services may be used to show you ads. If you choose to connect, data from Connected Content Apps will be processed to personalize your Search services experience or do things for you, like help you make a restaurant reservation. The same subset of data that is processed for personalization or to do things for you may also be used to show you ads in Search services. These ads may be personalized if your Google Ads Personalization setting is on.

Your choices here don’t change other personalization settings, whether other services exchange data, and whether Connected Content Apps use data to improve their own services.

You’re in control
You can change these choices in your Search settings and manage and delete data by visiting your AI Mode history or myactivity.google.com.
