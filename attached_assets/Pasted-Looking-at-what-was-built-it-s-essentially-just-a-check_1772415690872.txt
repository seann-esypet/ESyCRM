Looking at what was built — it's essentially just a checklist with a date and priority label. That's maybe 20% of what a real task system needs. Here's exactly what it's missing and what to tell the agent:

---

## What the Current Task System Can't Do

It can't be edited or deleted after creation. It has no task type (call vs. email vs. demo). It has no assigned rep. It has no reminder — so nothing actually notifies anyone. It has no notes field. It has no completion timestamp. It can't be linked back to trigger a follow-up task automatically. And "high/medium" priority is just a color tag with no function behind it.

---

## Paste This Directly Into the Replit Agent

> **Rebuild the Task system on the Deal Workspace. The current implementation is too basic. Here is everything it needs to do:**
>
> **1. Task Card — Display**
> Each task card must show:
> - Checkbox to mark complete (clicking it logs a completion timestamp and moves it to a "Completed" section)
> - Task Title
> - Task Type icon + label: 📞 Call | ✉️ Email | 🎥 Demo | 🔁 Follow-up | 🏪 Site Visit | 📋 Other
> - Assigned To (rep name)
> - Due Date + Due Time
> - Priority badge: High (red) | Medium (yellow) | Low (gray)
> - Reminder indicator: 🔔 if a reminder email is scheduled
> - Overdue state: if past due date and not complete, card gets a red left border and "OVERDUE" label
> - A "…" menu on hover with options: Edit | Delete | Duplicate | Mark Complete
>
> **2. Add/Edit Task Modal**
> When creating OR editing a task, the form must have:
> - Task Title (text input, required)
> - Task Type dropdown: Call | Email | Demo | Follow-up | Site Visit | Other
> - Priority: High | Medium | Low (radio or segmented button, required)
> - Due Date (date picker, required)
> - Due Time (time picker, optional)
> - Assigned To (dropdown of all sales rep users, defaults to logged-in user)
> - Reminder: None | 15 minutes before | 1 hour before | 1 day before | Custom date & time
> - Reminder Email: auto-filled with assigned rep's email, editable
> - Notes (optional textarea for context)
> - "After completing this task, auto-create a follow-up task" checkbox — if checked, show a field for the follow-up title and how many days out to schedule it
>
> **3. Task Sections — Sort & Group**
> Display tasks in this order, always:
> - 🔴 **Overdue** (past due, not complete) — red section header
> - 🟡 **Due Today** — yellow section header  
> - ⬜ **Upcoming** (future tasks, sorted by due date ascending)
> - ✅ **Completed** (collapsed by default, expandable, shows completion date/time)
>
> **4. Reminder Email**
> When a reminder fires, send an email to the assigned rep containing:
> - Subject: `⚑ Reminder: [Task Title] — [Contact First + Last Name]`
> - Contact name, company, phone number
> - Task type, due date/time, notes
> - A direct clickable link back to the Deal Workspace: `[Open Deal → Ted Crocket]`
> Use whatever email sender is already set up in this project (nodemailer / SendGrid / etc.)
>
> **5. Reminder Trigger**
> Add a cron job or scheduled function that runs every 15 minutes and:
> - Queries all incomplete tasks where `reminder_sent = false` and the reminder trigger time is in the past
> - Sends the reminder email
> - Sets `reminder_sent = true` so it doesn't fire again
>
> **6. Task Count Badge**
> In the right panel header, show a live count: "2 open · 1 overdue" — overdue count in red
>
> **7. Activity Feed Integration**
> When a task is created, auto-log it in the `deal_activities` table as a `task_created` event.
> When a task is completed, auto-log it as a `task_completed` event with the timestamp.
>
> **8. Database — deal_tasks table must have these columns:**
> `id, deal_id, contact_id, title, task_type, priority, due_date, due_time, assigned_to, assigned_email, reminder_timing, reminder_datetime, reminder_sent, notes, auto_followup (boolean), followup_title, followup_days_out, completed, completed_at, created_at, created_by`
>
> **Do NOT delete existing tasks — migrate the current data into the new schema.**

---

That gives the agent a complete, unambiguous spec. The auto-follow-up feature is especially powerful for your reps — when they complete "Call Ted," it can automatically create "Send demo link to Ted" due in 1 day without them having to think about it.
