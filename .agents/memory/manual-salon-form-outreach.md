name: Manual salon form outreach
description: User-approved boundary between verified form contacts and automated email outreach
---

For salon contact forms, prepare copyable personalized sequence drafts and ask a person to open the matched salon's site, solve any CAPTCHA, submit, and explicitly record the outcome. Never automate third-party form submissions or count a generated draft or verified form as a delivered message. Keep verified-email yield, usable-form yield, and distinct salons reached by either path separate.

**Why:** The user selected manual review and submission for third-party forms. Form availability does not prove successful delivery, and a contact link or booking-only flow is not a general message path.

**How to apply:** Any future form outreach, reporting, reminders, or contact-import work should preserve human submission and confirmation, configured sequence delays after recorded submissions, and isolation from automatic email sends and metrics.

Before counting a general-contact form, check its purpose, business identity and visible configuration notices—not just its inputs.

**Why:** Source review found complete-looking forms with unrelated-company consent, missing submission storage, and controls that only open a prefilled SMS app. None establishes a usable salon web-inquiry path.

**How to apply:** Exclude known broken/misbranded forms and booking, signup, recruitment or domain-purchase flows from general-form yield. Keep SMS links separate. Structural review still does not verify delivery.

Start contact-form drafting with existing saved outreach messages rather than a new paid AI provider.

**Why:** The user chose to try the free drafting option first. They explicitly said the salesperson would copy and paste the result.

**How to apply:** Provide editable salesperson name, reply email, subject and message, with copying controls. Do not claim Claude or another AI wrote a saved-template draft. Adding live AI generation is a separate decision.

The salesperson must be shown that owner research was performed before generating the form message.

**Why:** The user wants owner-name research visible before first-name wording is used.

**How to apply:** Require a recorded research attempt and show its result. Failed or no-name research can still produce a business-team greeting; an attempt alone cannot establish a person's identity. Keep approved ownership and personal-greeting opt-in separate, and never count drafting or copying as submission.
