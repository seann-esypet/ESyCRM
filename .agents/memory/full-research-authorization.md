name: Full Research authorization
description: User-approved initial hourly/list limits and the distinction between building the queue and authorizing paid research.
---

The user approved starting limits per uploaded list of 20 businesses per hour, 40 paid provider requests per hour, and 200 paid provider requests total.

**Why:** These are cautious initial limits. The user asked what the 200-request total meant and accepted the explanation that it is a safety stop, not 200 businesses, 200 emails, $200, or guaranteed whole-list coverage.

**How to apply:** Treat every uploaded state list independently. Reaching the list-wide cap requires explicit approval of a higher cumulative total; do not reset past spending on restart or recovery.

Approving these settings in conversation authorized building the controls, not starting live paid searches. Full Research is optional and must be explicitly activated inside the app.

**Why:** The user-approved scope separates implementing the combined workflow from spending on real research.

**How to apply:** Verify with isolated records and mocked providers. Do not automatically activate on upload, publish, or application startup, and do not restart the closed legacy research pilot.
