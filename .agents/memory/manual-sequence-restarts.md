---
name: Manual sequence restarts
description: Product decision allowing future manual CRM sequences despite completed historical outreach.
---

Past sequence history alone must not block a user from manually scheduling a future CRM sequence, including older completed records without a retained recipient address.

**Why:** The user was blocked while scheduling another sequence and explicitly asked: “make it so it does not block a future sequence, Right now I am blocked.”

**How to apply:** Treat manual starts as intentional new outreach, not as first-contact automation. Preserve historical records rather than deleting them or inventing the earlier recipient. Keep duplicate-active-outreach and unresolved-send-attempt safeguards. A new run's follow-ups should depend on that run's sends and replies, not on completed past runs. Reviewed lead-import/first-contact flows retain their separate eligibility rules; this change was requested for manually scheduling CRM contacts.
