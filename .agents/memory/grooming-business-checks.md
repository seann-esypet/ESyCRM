The user approved separate Full Research checks for whether the exact business/address offers pet grooming services and whether it is a grooming shop. These are separate questions: a pet store, clinic, boarding business or mobile provider may offer grooming without being a dedicated shop.

**Why:** The user asked both questions and approved adding both evidence-backed checks.

**How to apply:** Keep the findings distinct and reviewable. Missing information is Unknown, not No; conflicting evidence needs review. Do not automatically approve, discard, import or contact a business based on these checks.

The user wants Gemini AI to ask these questions, “or at least look for gemini answering those questions,” to take advantage of free AI answers.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested AI assistance, but direct Gemini access and free API availability were not established.

**How to apply:** Search-provided Google AI answers may be retained as clearly unverified hints, with cited live pages independently checked against the exact business/address. Do not claim direct Gemini calls or guaranteed free AI access when only search answers are available. Adding a separately billed provider requires an explicit spending decision; building checks does not authorize live research.

Found contact names and email addresses must be prominent in the Leads research/review UI, with darker and/or thicker section borders.

**Why:** The user said important information was hard to find and the UI was too light.

**How to apply:** Preserve clear found-contact and email emphasis, distinguish unverified suggestions from approved information, and never display a business name as a discovered person.
